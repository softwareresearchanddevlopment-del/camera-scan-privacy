This Privacy Policy explains how Camera Scan: All Doc, PDF Tools ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your documents remain under your full control.

1. On-Device Document Processing

All document scanning, PDF creation, editing, splitting, merging, compression, watermarking, digital signatures, Office file viewing, and PowerPoint conversions are performed 100% locally on your device. We do not upload, store, view, or share your scanned photos, PDFs, or office documents on any external server.

2. Permissions We Request

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly when you open the document scanner to capture photos of physical documents and convert them into PDFs or images. No camera feed or image is transmitted off your device.
  • Storage & Media Access (Android Photo Picker & SAF): We use Android's system Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework (SAF) so the app only accesses the specific images, PDFs, or folders you explicitly choose to import or edit.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely to load banner advertisements via Google AdMob.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays non-intrusive banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process limited device information (such as your Android Advertising ID / AD_ID, general location inferred from IP address, and ad interaction diagnostics) in order to serve and measure ads.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps here: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

4. Data Sharing & User Accounts

The App does not require user registration or login, and we do not maintain any user database. Your files are shared only when you explicitly tap the Share or Print action inside the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general utility tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
